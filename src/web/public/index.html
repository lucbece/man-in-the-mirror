Connection

The bot needs its own account on Discord, and a token that proves it's yours. Two minutes, one page:

  1. Open the Discord Developer Portal and click New Application. The name you give it is the name people see in the member list.
  2. Open the Bot tab → Reset Token → Copy. Discord shows the token once and never again; if you lose it, just reset for a new one.
  3. Paste it below and hit Save & apply.
  4. Back in the portal, open Installation and enable the bot and applications.commands scopes plus the Connect and Speak permissions. Open the link it generates to invite the bot to your server.

No privileged intents needed — leave those toggles alone. Secrets are saved to data/config.json, readable only by you on macOS/Linux; on Windows it inherits the folder's permissions.

Voice channel

Listening

While listening, the bot keeps recent conversation in memory so it has context when you speak to it. Nothing is ever written to disk, and anything older than the window is dropped.

Required for the wake phrase, and the difference between answering in seconds and answering in half a minute. It does mean paying to transcribe everything said in the channel, not just what's buffered when someone asks.

Discord shows a deafened icon next to the bot whenever it isn't listening, so everyone in the channel can see its state without taking your word for it. People should know when it can hear them.

Thinking

Works with either provider. Without it, the agent can only reason over what it heard in the channel, and will say so rather than guess. Adds a second or two to each answer because it actually goes and looks.

Replies are capped at a few sentences and stripped of anything unspeakable — no lists, no markdown, no links. It answers in whichever language you asked in.

Speaking

Speech is sent to Discord as Opus and played untouched, which is the fastest path and avoids re-encoding every reply. The trade is that this app can't change its own loudness — adjust it in Discord instead: right-click the bot in the member list and use the user volume slider. That's per-listener anyway, which is better than one setting for everyone.
